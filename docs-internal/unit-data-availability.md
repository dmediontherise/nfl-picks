# NFL Unit Data Availability & Scheme Tendency Audit

This document audits data availability for team unit stats, situational splits, and scheme tendencies sourced from free `nflverse` and `FTN Data` feeds.

## Availability Status Matrix

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Rush Offense & Defense (EPA/play, success rate, yards/carry)** | `LIVE-2026` | `pbp/play_by_play_{YYYY}.csv.gz` | `epa`, `success`, `rushing_yards`, `rush_attempt`, `play_type` | Updated daily in-season through week 4+. |

| **Red Zone Efficiency (Trips, TD rate, points/trip)** | `LIVE-2026` | `pbp/play_by_play_{YYYY}.csv.gz` | `yardline_100 <= 20`, `fixed_drive`, `fixed_drive_result` | Trips defined as distinct `game_id`+`fixed_drive` reaching <= 20. |

| **Live 2026 Personnel, Formations & Coverages** | `UNAVAILABLE` | N/A | N/A | nflverse `pbp_participation_2026.csv` is not published during the active season. |

## 2026 Data Gap

During the active NFL regular season, `pbp_participation_{YYYY}.csv` is **not published** by nflverse (HTTP 404). For instance, the 2025 participation release was published on 2026-02-10 following the conclusion of the 2025 season.

Consequently, while unit efficiency (EPA/play, success rate, yards per carry/attempt, red zone, blitz rates) is available `LIVE-2026`, scheme tendencies (offensive personnel shares, formations, defensive front classification, coverage types) must use the **2025 season as a prior**. If `pbp_participation_2026.csv` is ever published, the pipeline automatically detects the HTTP 200 response and produces current-season scheme splits alongside the 2025 prior.
